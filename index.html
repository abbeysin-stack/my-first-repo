Privacy and reporting notes

Extra CSV fields, including names and student IDs, are discarded. Student codes and predefined absence descriptions are retained in the authenticated staff register and coded CSV export; they never enter leadership emails. Free-text personal or medical notes are not stored. Codes must be random and unrelated to real identifiers. This is pseudonymization, not guaranteed anonymization. The original file remains on your device; its contents exist temporarily in browser memory during processing. No analytics or persistent browser storage are used. Coded records are sent only to this application’s server; Microsoft school sign-in uses Microsoft’s identity service. Division labels must be approved by you and must not identify a person. Aggregation does not guarantee anonymity, especially for small status groups.

One row must represent one student on one day. Duplicate student/date rows in a CSV are rejected; subsequent imports update existing student/date entries. Missing students cannot be detected without a complete roster. CSV and manual entry are supported; Excel workbooks must first be exported to CSV. Server records persist when you reload or sign out. Exported CSV files remain on your device. Demo data is fictional, stays in memory, and never enters the shared database.
