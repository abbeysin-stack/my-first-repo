Privacy and reporting notes

The authenticated staff register stores first names with last initials, registration numbers, internal student codes, and absence reasons and notes. These are personal data, not anonymous records. Internal codes are generated independently of registration numbers. Leadership emails contain division aggregates only; coded CSV exports omit display names, registration numbers, and written notes, but still contain sensitive pseudonymous attendance and reason codes. Named roster imports retain only the supported identity fields; unrecognized extra columns are discarded. Written notes are not automatically scrubbed for personal data. No analytics or persistent browser storage are used. Microsoft school sign-in protects staff access. Aggregation does not guarantee anonymity, especially for small status groups.

One row must represent one student on one day. Duplicate student/date rows in a CSV are rejected; subsequent imports update existing student/date entries. Missing students cannot be detected without a complete roster. CSV and manual entry are supported; Excel workbooks must first be exported to CSV. Server records persist when you reload or sign out. Exported CSV files remain on your device. Demo data is fictional, stays in memory, and never enters the shared database.
